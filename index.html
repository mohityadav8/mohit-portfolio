Publications & preprints

LLM alignment Zenodo preprint, v2, Sept 2026

DPO vs. RLHF: An Empirical Comparison of Alignment Techniques for Large Language Models

Mohit Yadav — Chandigarh University, 2026, CC BY 4.0

GPT-2 (124M) aligned with DPO (β = 0.1) and with RLHF (SFT, Bradley–Terry reward model, PPO) on 160,800 Anthropic HH-RLHF preference pairs, on a single 6 GB RTX 3050. Compares alignment quality, reward accuracy, cost, latency and alignment tax. DPO reached 71% reward accuracy (from 41%) and a 0.640 reward margin with no reward model, training in 16.6 min against 11.9 min for SFT. Released with 9 figures, 17 tables, training logs and reproduction scripts.

GPT-2DPORLHFPPOReward modeling
